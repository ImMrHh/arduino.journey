## About the Curriculum

Arduino Journey is a 10-lesson hands-on electronics and programming curriculum for middle school students. Each lesson is designed to fit a 40-minute class session and is structured for collaborative groups of 3 students.

Students begin with no experience and end with a working MP3 player built from components, wired on a breadboard, and programmed in Arduino C++.
